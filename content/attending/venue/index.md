## Traveling

### Getting to Cyprus

Cyprus is a full member of the EU and the Eurozone. All EU citizens can travel with their ID/Passport.

Cyprus is a candidate country bound to join the Schengen Area. Residents of non-EU countries who hold valid multiple-entry Schengen visas must first pass through a full Schengen member territory in order to utilize the visa in Cyprus.

### Entry Points

Larnaca Airport (LCA) and Paphos Airport (PFO) support 80+ international airlines and provide 150+ direct flight connections.

* **Larnaca Airport (LCA):** 55 km from the venue (~45 min drive)
* **Paphos Airport (PFO):** 70 km from the venue (~55 min drive)

### Getting Around

Distances in Cyprus are relatively small, making transportation from one place to another straightforward. Note that Cypriots drive on the left and vehicles are right-hand drive. The roads throughout the island are in good condition, and signposting is very clear.

Transportation options include public buses/airport shuttles, car hire/taxi, electric scooters/bikes, and bicycles.
