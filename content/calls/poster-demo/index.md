title: "Call For Posters and Demos"

---

The Posters and Demos Track of the European Semantic Web Conference (ESWC) 2027 welcomes submissions presenting late-breaking results, ongoing research, innovative ideas, and early-stage prototypes within the Semantic Web domain. This track encourages discussions and interaction between researchers and practitioners, facilitating feedback on emerging work and demonstrations of cutting-edge technologies.

We welcome submissions that span the broad spectrum of Semantic Web research and applications, including but not limited to:
- Knowledge Graphs & Ontologies: Representation, reasoning, modularization, integration, and evolution.
- AI & Large Language Models: Neuro-symbolic AI, retrieval-augmented generation, and explainability.
- Natural Language Processing & Understanding: Entity linking, text generation, and semantic search.
- Machine Learning for Semantic Technologies: Representation learning, embeddings, and hybrid approaches. 
- Data Quality, FAIR Principles & Trust: Data governance, privacy, bias, and security.
- Decentralized & Federated Systems: Linked data, streaming knowledge graphs, and IoT applications.
- Semantic Applications: eScience, life sciences, cultural heritage, smart cities, and industrial AI.
- Agents & Multi-Agent Systems: LLM-based agents, knowledge graphs as agent memory, and semantic interoperability between agents.
- Tools & Interaction: User interfaces, visualization, query and exploration tools.

Contributions should be clearly related to the Semantic Web or have an impact in the field of semantics. ESWC adheres to the principles and guidelines stated in the [ACM Policy on Authorship](https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/new-acm-policy-on-authorship) specifically about “Criteria for Authorship” regarding the use of Generative AI technologies for authoring papers. Authors must disclose the use of generative AI tools in a separate ‘Use of Generative AI’ section.

## Review Criteria

Posters will be reviewed according to the following criteria:
- Relevance to the Semantic Web and the topics of ESWC 2027
- Novelty and potential impact of the idea or (preliminary) results
- Soundness of the approach and, where available, of the preliminary evaluation
- Clarity of presentation, including a clear statement of the contribution and the current status of the work
- Potential to stimulate discussion and to benefit from feedback at the conference

Demos will be reviewed according to the following criteria:
- Relevance to the Semantic Web and the topics of ESWC 2027
- Novelty and significance of the demonstrated system, tool, or interface
- Technical maturity of the system and its readiness to be demonstrated live at the conference
- Added value of the live demonstration beyond the paper, i.e., what attendees will see, do, and take away
- Accessibility of the online demo or video, and availability of code and data where applicable
- Clarity of presentation
- For companion demos: added value beyond the accompanying main track paper

## Optional Poster Feedback

Authors of poster submissions are invited to upload a draft of their poster along with their paper. The poster draft is not part of the review and does not influence the acceptance decision. Instead, reviewers will provide feedback on it to help authors prepare their poster for the conference.

We hope this is especially helpful for early-career researchers presenting a poster for the first time, as well as for authors from other domains who are new to the Semantic Web community. However, all authors are invited to make use of this opportunity. The poster draft does not count towards the page limit and will not be published in the proceedings.

## Presentation Format 
Accepted posters and demos will be presented in an interactive session, allowing direct exchanges between authors and conference attendees. Additionally, all presenters will participate in a “Minute Madness” session to briefly highlight their contributions. Submissions will also be eligible for Best Poster and Best Demo Awards.


## Submission Guidelines 
- All submissions must be in English. 
- Submissions must be in PDF. Springer Publications expects the LaTeX source for the camera ready.
- Submissions must be formatted in the style of the Springer Publications format for Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS). For details on the LNCS style, see [Springer’s Author Instructions](https://www.springer.com/us/computer-science/lncs/conference-proceedings-guidelines).
- Poster and demo submissions are at most 5 pages long, including references and the Generative AI statement. 
- Demo submissions must additionally provide a description of the demonstration that will be given at the conference (which may include screenshots and must include either a link to an online demo or to a video presenting it). 
- Poster submissions can optionally include a draft of the poster as a separate PDF (see ‘Optional Poster Feedback’ above). 
- Submissions must clearly demonstrate relevance to the Semantic Web and the topics of interest of ESWC 2027. 
Papers that exceed the given page length or do not follow the LNCS guidelines will be desk rejected without review. 
- The authors of accepted papers at the main tracks (i.e. research, resource, and in-use) are encouraged to submit a companion demo. A companion demo must fulfil all requirements for demo submissions. Aside from such cases, work that is under review at or accepted by any other conference, workshop, or track of ESWC will be rejected. 
- Authors’ names and affiliations should be included.
- Submission is done through EasyChair [(https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=eswc2027)](https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=eswc2027). When logging in, select the “Posters and Demos” track and indicate whether your submission is a poster or a demo, and whether it is a companion demo.


## Proceedings and Registration

Accepted poster and demo papers will appear in supplementary post-conference proceedings to be published by Springer in the series Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS). Camera-ready versions shall be available online during the conference. 

Participants with an accepted poster or demonstration must register for the conference and present their work during the Poster and Demos Session. Space will be allocated for each participant. In case of any special requirements, the organizers should be contacted in advance of the conference.
